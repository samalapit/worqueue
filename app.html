    What is Worqueue?

    Worqueue is a job application tracker that helps you keep all your applications in one place. Add companies, roles, salaries, dates, links, notes, and current status so you can see your job search clearly and stay organized.

    Website by sabm.

    Private and local

    Worqueue works in your browser without an account or login. Your applications, profile and resume are saved on this device and are not sent to a server.

    Back up your data

    Browser data can be cleared, so export a backup before changing devices or clearing your browser. Import that backup later to restore your applications and profile details. Resume files are not included.

    Try it with sample data

    Load a few made-up applications to see how the dashboard, follow-ups and filters work. Sample entries can be removed in one click and never touch your own records.
