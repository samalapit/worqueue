What is Worqueue?

Worqueue is a job application tracker that helps you keep all your applications in one place. Add companies, roles, salaries, dates, links, notes, and current status so you can see your job search clearly and stay organized.

Website by sabm.

Private and local

Worqueue works in your browser without an account or login. Your application details are saved on this device and are not sent to a server.

Back up your data

Browser data can be cleared, so export a backup before changing devices or clearing your browser. Import that backup later to restore your applications.
